Encryption Standards

All transmissions are secured via end-to-end encryption. The Ministry utilizes military-grade cryptographic protocols ensuring sector communications remain strictly within the grid. Session tokens are rotated on every login.

Data Collection

The Ministry logs only essential metadata: operative handles, transmission timestamps, and visual assets. Location telemetry is anonymized and auto-purged upon session termination. No tracking cookies are deployed.

Retention & Purge

Broadcasts and ephemeral data are auto-redacted after 30 days. Permanent archives in the Vault remain under Directorate jurisdiction until manual decommissioning. Operatives may request full profile deletion via the Directorate.

Third-Party Integrity

Zero-sharing policy. M.O.G. assets are never traded, sold, or exposed to external surveillance entities. The grid is self-contained and sovereign. Supabase infrastructure is used solely for authentication and data persistence.

Your Rights

Operatives retain the right to access, correct, or request deletion of their personal data at any time. Contact the Directorate via the channels below. Requests are actioned within 48 hours.
