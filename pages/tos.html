Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026.03.10

1. Grid Access

All operatives must maintain active clearance status. Access to the Grid is granted on a need-to-know basis and can be revoked at any time by Directorate.

2. Communication Protocol

All transmissions are monitored and encrypted. Sensitive information should never be shared over unsecured channels. Operatives are responsible for maintaining operational security.

3. Asset Ownership

All visual assets, audio recordings, and documents transmitted through Ministry systems become property of the Ministry of Groove. Distribution without authorization is prohibited.

4. Directorate Override

Directorate maintains supreme authority over all systems. Override access is restricted to clearance level 5 and above. Unauthorized override attempts will trigger security protocols.

5. Termination

Failure to comply with these terms will result in immediate termination of access and potential disciplinary action. The Ministry reserves the right to modify these terms without notice.
