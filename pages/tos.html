Terms of Service

Effective Date: 2026.03.15

1. Grid Access

All operatives must maintain active clearance status. Access to the Grid is granted on a need-to-know basis and remains at the sole discretion of the Directorate.

2. Communication Protocol

Transmissions are encrypted but monitored for sector safety. Operatives are strictly forbidden from sharing Ministry-exclusive frequencies with unverified external channels.

3. Asset Sovereignty

Any visual data, audio logs, or intelligence metadata processed by the Grid becomes the sovereign property of the Ministry. Unauthorized extraction or duplication is a breach of contract.

4. Directorate Override

System hierarchy is absolute. Directorate maintains override authority (Level 5 Clearance) over all operative interfaces. Attempting to bypass these gates will trigger an auto-redaction of the operative's profile.

5. Termination

Breach of protocol results in immediate grid-blackout for the offending operative. The Ministry reserves the right to modify these tactical terms at any time to preserve grid integrity.
