1. Grid Access

All operatives must maintain active clearance status. Access to the Grid is granted on a need-to-know basis and remains at the sole discretion of the Directorate. Clearance levels are subject to review and may be revoked at any time.

2. Communication Protocol

Transmissions are encrypted but monitored for sector safety. Operatives are strictly forbidden from sharing Ministry-exclusive frequencies with unverified external channels. Unauthorized dissemination constitutes a breach of this agreement.

3. Asset Sovereignty

Any visual data, audio logs, or intelligence metadata processed by the Grid becomes the sovereign property of the Ministry. Unauthorized extraction, duplication, or redistribution is a breach of contract and may result in immediate grid-blackout.

4. Directorate Override

System hierarchy is absolute. The Directorate maintains override authority (Level 5 Clearance) over all operative interfaces. Attempting to bypass security gates will trigger automated redaction of the offending operative's profile.

5. Conduct Standards

All operatives are expected to conduct themselves with professionalism befitting the Ministry. Harassment, impersonation, or conduct that undermines the integrity of the network will result in immediate suspension pending Directorate review.

6. Termination

Breach of protocol results in immediate grid-blackout for the offending operative. The Ministry reserves the right to modify these tactical terms at any time to preserve grid integrity. Continued use of the system constitutes acceptance of the current terms.
