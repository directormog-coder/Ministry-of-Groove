The Sanction Clause

The Directorate reserves the absolute right to terminate the signal of any operative (Ban) who violates the peace of the Grid. Banned operatives will be redirected to the "Signal Lost" (404) sector indefinitely.

Prohibited Frequency

  • Unauthorized access to the Directorate Hub Terminal.
  • Harassment of fellow operatives within the Archives.
  • Manipulation of Signal Strength (XP) values.
