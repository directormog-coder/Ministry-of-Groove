Privacy Directive (POPIA)

Last Synchronized: March 2026

01. Data Collection

The Ministry of Groove collects only essential operative data: email addresses for authentication and interaction logs (XP/Rankings) to maintain the Grid's integrity.

02. Purpose of Processing

Data is utilized strictly for access control, leaderboard functionality, and security notifications. No data is shared with external third parties without explicit Directorate authorization.

03. Operative Rights

In accordance with South African law, all operatives have the right to request access to their stored data or demand the permanent deletion of their "Signal" from the Vault.
