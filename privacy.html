Data Protection

Military-grade encryption

Encryption Standards

All data is secured using AES-256 encryption. Communications are end-to-end encrypted and cannot be intercepted by third parties. Your operational security is our priority.

Data Collection

We collect only essential operational data: handle, transmission logs, and uploaded assets. Location data is anonymized and never stored permanently. No personal identifiers are retained.

Data Retention

Messages are ephemeral and auto-purge after 30 days. Gallery assets remain until manually deleted by the operative or Directorate. Access logs are maintained for 90 days for security purposes.

Third Parties

The Ministry of Groove does not sell, trade, or transfer your data to external entities. All data remains within secure Ministry servers.

Your Rights

Operatives may request data deletion at any time. Contact Directorate for data export or deletion requests. Response time is within 72 hours.
